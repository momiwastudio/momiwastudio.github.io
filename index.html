// ABOUT

HELLO,
I'M SHERINA.

From the dense green landscapes of Indonesia to contributing to Melbourne / Naarm's growing communities, my journey is rooted in one belief: great spaces build great communities.

A Master of Architecture graduate from RMIT, I've shaped stadiums and masterplans with HKS / Harwood K. Smith — the firm behind Optus Stadium and SoFi Stadium, future home of the 2028 Olympics.

I design with strategy and empathy, combining a passion for development and architecture. From intimate residential projects to large-scale city-shaping developments, I understand that architecture is more than shelter — it's a stage where relationships, learning, and communities flourish.

Outside the studio: hiking trails, bouldering walls, fishing lines, and late-night games. Every favourite moment is a shared one.
